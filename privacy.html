Privacy

This site records page visits for basic audience measurement and security. Records may include your IP address, approximate location supplied by Cloudflare, browser and device information, referrer, requested page, allowlisted campaign tags, and the time of the visit.

First-party cookies assign a random visitor identifier for up to 30 days and a 30-minute session identifier. Small first-party events record page visibility milestones and internal link clicks so automated traffic can be distinguished from engaged visits. No keystrokes, mouse movements, advertising identifiers, or cross-site tracking are collected.

Other query strings are not retained. Security checks may classify and block obvious automated scanners. Audit records are kept for up to 30 days and are not sold. They are used only to understand site traffic, provide visitor notifications, detect abuse, and operate the site.
