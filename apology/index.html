Apology

Emma, Sweet girl, I was so mean to you. I felt you reach for me. But we echoed an old betrayal. I held that against you. I made you feel unsafe, unwanted, unheard, like an object. My wound is mine to control. To heal. Not yours to survive. I'm sorry I let it reach you. I was so busy protecting myself that I stopped listening to you. I replay your words that day. Now I hear them. I didn't show you I cared when it mattered most. I believed I couldn't be hurt. I could. I was. I am. I unraveled. And then I gave you new reasons to keep your distance. Some days I didn’t want us to be together. Some days I did. That isn’t what I’m grieving. It's that I lost you altogether. The silence. I don't know if I'll ever hear your voice again. I wonder if the worst of me is all you remember. If sending this does anything but cause us pain. I care about you. You matter to me. I am sorry. And yeah, I miss our stupid banter. Best part of my day. Matt

Vulnerability

I was petrified of being vulnerable. Somewhere I learned to not be vulnerable precisely when the moment requires. I am practicing it now. I flirt with other women in front of the one I care about. To hide my need, to pretend that I'd be okay without them. I thought it was unique to a particular situation from my past, its not. When we looked deeply into each others eyes, or embraced, I wouldn't give any more of me. So many moments I regret not speaking softly to you, not lingering with you. You told me that fateful day how long it takes to build a good relationship. I wasn't listening to you. I didn't empathise with the situation you found yourself in. I gave you an ultimatum because I wanted relief. My upset at our circumstances, my insecurities, my inability to let myself be vulnerable. My new medication exasperated these feelings, it did not create them. We surged towards each other after I left you in the yard. Then I put on my salesman's face as we walked together instead of being myself. You felt me pushing into your life, you felt cornered. No wonder you reacted like you did. I was a tyrant. I told you that you were playing a game with me, instead of realizing how I'd made you feel. At the final moment instead of asking what we meant, I simply stated that you touched me. I showed you no sweetness or kindness. You, the person I cared about, got coldness and ambivalence. I was sweeter to Makenna's dog. There is a hurt kid in me who is terrified of abandonment. The people I loved began leaving me from a young age. It followed me into my first serious relationship. We would both pretend to leave each other. And both elaborately protest. When you disappeared, it was quick and brutal. I don't blame you. Slowly I realized how deeply I cared about you, and I felt truly abandoned for the first time in years. I came undone, I protested. The meme you sent me meant more than you probably knew. At the time I hadn't even begun to understand why. It told that frightened part of me that we were cool. I was too busy crashing out to notice, and I returned a small kindness with a new push. All this frightened child inside me wants is to know that he has not been abandoned. Its not yours to fix.
